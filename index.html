How to use this tool

Understanding helps you identify the variables, and know their letters and units.
Formula - once you understand the variables, this walks you through using them in equations.

1. Start with Understanding

Pick a topic above (e.g. "Understanding Mechanics"), then select the sub-topic (e.g. Harmonics) from the drop-down.

There are three levels:

  • Learner
  • Confident
  • Expert

Figure out where you are at, and work through the drills until you can confidently identify the items. You can skip ahead or back at any time.

Some areas (like Angular Mechanics) have explainer diagrams on each card to help you remember ideas. Others have a general "show picture" button.

2. Then try Formula

Once you've worked through Understanding, click All Tools to come back to this page and choose the Formula option for your topic.

Select the area, e.g. Hooke's Law, from the drop-down, and work through the levels (Learner, Confident, Expert) as before.

Bugs

This is a work in progress - there will be the odd bug. Mechanics and Waves have had the most attention so far. You can just skip over it, or try pasting the question into Google to find out what is going on (brackets matter!). Happy to hear about possible fixes.
